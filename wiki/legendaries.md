# Special mons & solo mode

## Special categories (solo-only)

Legendaries, mythicals , and — in later gens — paradox / Gmax / mega-evolution
forms are **solo-team-only** starters. For Gen 1 v0: **Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Mewtwo**
(legendaries) + **Mew** (mythical).

## Unlock flow — "catch it, play it"

- A run's **final boss can be a special mon** (random chance — rate TBD, possibly rising with Ascension/Dex).
- Beat it, then attempt the catch at a **low catch rate**.
- Catching it permanently unlocks it as a **solo-mode starter**. No separate Dex-completion gate.

## Solo mode

Triggered by picking a special mon, **or** by toggling solo on any regular starter (a
self-imposed challenge). Rules:

- **Team capped at 1.** No second slot.
- **Switching disabled** — no switch button, no 1-energy switch cost. You are this mon all run.
- **Captures go to the Dex only** — they never join the team.

## Balance

Special mons have high base stats, so they hit hard from L5 — intentionally. They're
balanced by:

- **Solo constraint** — no STAB-switching, no team coverage, no chip-and-capture support. The whole game's core lever is removed.
- **Slow XP curves** — most legendaries level slower, falling behind in `levelScale`.
- Difficulty roughly equals **A8–A10** of regular mode even at A0. Composes with Ascensions for the truly masochistic.

The design goal: "You want to play Mewtwo? Here's a _challenge_." Not "spam Mewtwo because broken."
