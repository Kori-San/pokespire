# Shinies

- **Encounter rate:** `1/256` per wild encounter (vs canonical 1/8192 — special but reachable).
- **Catch bonus:** ×5 on the species's catch rate (`shinyBonus` in the capture formula).
- **Sprite:** alt-color BW animated from PokéAPI shiny URLs (resolver handles normal + shiny).
- **Unlock:** first shiny capture per species records `meta.unlockedShinies: dexId[]`.
- **Selection:** StarterSelect shows a Shiny toggle for any unlocked species — purely cosmetic, no stat differences. A vanity reward.
